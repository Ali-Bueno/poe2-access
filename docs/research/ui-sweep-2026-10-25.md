## Measurements

- **Options sections** (`GameLayout.OptionsSection`): row → list → page → section. Byte +0x368 is set on the active
  section, qword +0x370 is its highlighted row, disabled rows included. Gráficos went 0 → 11 down the first section
  (rows 1, 5 and 9 disabled, the manager's activation target `M+0x748` reading 0 on them), then the flag moved to the
  second section, whose row started at 0. Proven at run time on a focused row (`ControlGroup.OptionsSection`, weak).
- **Quest map depth** (`GameLayout.WorldMap`): no byte of the toggle button nor of its four ancestors changes with a
  press; a snapshot of the 2881 elements of the Misiones panel (0xC00 bytes each) across two presses found one field
  flipping both ways: the canvas (`WorldScreen` > `map_control` > child 0) +0x566, 1 on the surface, 0 underground.
  The toggle is known by its click sounds `DepthToggleDown.ogg` / `DepthToggleUp.ogg`. Found again after an update by
  watching it change (`ControlGroup.MapDepth`).
- **Quest reward icons**: `Art/2DItems/QuestItems/Reward<Kind>.dds` — ArtificersOrb, AtlasPassive, Book, Charms,
  ConsumableBuff, CraftingBench, Flasks, Gold, PrecursorTablet, SkillGem, SpiritDraught, SupportGem (0.5.5e bundle
  index). Spanish names follow the game's item classes (ItemClasses.dat: "Gemas de asistencia", "Viales", "Tablilla").
- **Skill row sockets**: `support_gem_container` holds only the sockets the gem has (two on a new gem); the greyed
  places after them are part of the row's frame, not elements.
- **Challenges rows**: row > content > (`top_frame`: `toggle_button`, `title_bar`) + `collapsable_frame`, visible
  only while open.
