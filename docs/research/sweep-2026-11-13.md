# Sweep of 2026-11-13 (night): the play log, odd play simulated, a live pass

Asked by the owner: hunt bugs and quality of life, see how the mod behaves under different play. Four passes: the play
log of 2026-11-12 19:31 to 11-13 18:54 (66,740 lines, 14,639 spoken; no exception or fault in it), the pure classes driven
with timelines the tests did not have (values at a threshold, states renewed, empty and doubled input), the speech flow
of the same log, and a live pass on kehgan (Monk 11, the Act 1 town) with the virtual pad. Every row was fixed that night
unless it says otherwise; each choice is one constant or phrase the owner can overrule.

## Memory and frame cost on a very large map

The Trial of the Sekhemas floor (`Sanctum_1_Foyer_1`, 64,080 sub-tiles, 33.9 million cells; every other area that day
had 7,000 to 15,400 sub-tiles) took the host from 503 MB to 2,958 MB in 24 minutes and the frame from 1.5 to 3.5 ms,
with 22 slow ticks a minute. Causes, measures and what changed: [performance](performance.md).
