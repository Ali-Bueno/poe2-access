## The endgame since 0.5 (one community guide for the names, PCGamesN for the structure)

The Atlas starts at its centre; Breach, Expedition, Ritual and Delirium each have a starting zone, a quest chain, an NPC, Atlas passive
points and a pinnacle boss; Abyss and the Vaal Temple are core. Three Masters of the Atlas (Jado, Hilda, Doryani) give twelve nodes each.
A precursor Fortress at the centre (Enigma Chambers → Crisis Fragments → the Arbiter of Ash, now a quest boss); Citadels need tier 15
waystones and drop the Origin Cradle and Spark; Precursor Towers are mini-boss maps; the Origin Core starts the Arbiter of Divinity, the
apex fight. Precursor Tablets add a mechanic to every map in a tower's reach.

## Each mechanic as it is played now

- **Breach** (reworked 0.5): a Breach is purple fire on the map; it grows and spawns Hiveborn; a bar at the bottom of the screen is the
  time left, kills extend it, at 100% it becomes a Stabilised Breach. Chain: 300 Breach Splinters → Revelatory Wombgift → the Genesis Tree
  (Monastery of the Keepers, NPC Ailith) → Breachstone → Breach Stronghold → Hive (defend Ailith), Hive Fortress, Hive Colony (the Breachlords)
  → Xesht. The Genesis Tree window: four branches, each fed a Wombgift and Hiveblood at its large node.
- **Ritual** (reworked 0.5, in the campaign since 0.5.5): activate the altar, kill the raised monsters inside the circle for Tribute, spend it
  on Favours (reroll; defer an item to a later Ritual at a discount; a red outline is an item that will not come back). Since 0.5 the
  favours are Uniques and Omens and leftover Tribute buys an audience with the King in the Mists (NPC Aoife, Caer Tarth; the Rite of the
  Nameless is a chain of five maps; pinnacle the Bodach). 0.5.5: two clusters of Ritual Effigies an Act and one an Interlude, each on the
  area's boss, who comes back in the next Ritual of the cluster; Sacred Blooms add the Viridian Wildwood, where Wisps are followed.
- **Delirium** (reworked 0.5): walk through the mirror at the start of a map; fog covers the map and a gauge shows at the bottom of the
  screen; the fog moves and ends the encounter when it catches the player; shards on the gauge spawn packs (green stronger, pink richer,
  red another dimension to escape, purple harder and richer). Liquid Emotions anoint amulets. Chain: Elder Maddox → the Withered Willow →
  Grand Mirrors → fog banks to 100% → the Simulacrum of Delusion (seven waves) → Tangmazu.
- **Expedition** (reworked 0.5, core from Act 4 since 0.5.5): place explosives from the Detonator, each within reach of the last, each with
  a circle that takes in the markers inside it; then set off the Detonator and fight what is dug up. 15 explosives since 0.5.3. On a
  controller (one forum report, older): placement mode by holding the left stick in and pressing Select, the cursor on the right stick,
  and players report the cursor sticking. Runes of Aldur: Kingsmarch (Rog, Dannig, Farrow), Logbooks open islands, Verisium and Runic
  Remnants, bosses Medved, Vorana, Uhtred, Olroth.
- **Abyss** (0.3, reworked 0.5): Pits with fissures leading out; killing the monsters by a fissure closes it; all closed, the Pit opens,
  Abyssals pour out, then an Abyssal Trove rises. Sometimes an entrance to the Abyssal Depths (a small zone with a rare boss, Troves, the
  only source of Abyssal Eyes and Lineage Supports). Abyssal Bones desecrate an item and the Well of Souls reveals three modifiers.
- **Vaal Temple** (0.4, core 0.5): Ancient Beacons (six in Act 3, six in the Interludes; Vaal Beacons in maps): clear around one, step on
  it for an Energised Crystal; six wake the Temple Console at Atziri's Temple, where a temple of chained rooms is built; the Architect,
  then Atziri.
- **Essences**: a monster held in a crystal; freeing it gives the Essence. **Strongboxes**: magic, rare or unique boxes whose modifiers
  say what opening them does. **Shrines**: clear around one and use it for a buff. **Rogue Exiles**: rare exile monsters. **Azmeri
  spirits and wisps**: since 0.5.5 a spirit has a chance to manifest; how they are followed was not found on the web.
- **Trial of Chaos**: [its own document](trial-of-chaos.md). **Corrupted Nexus**: a failed map can be tried again (0.5.5).

## Version 1.0 (announced at ExileCon, 2026-11-06)

- **11 December 2026**, PC, PS5 and Xbox Series, free to play.
- **Acts 5 and 6** (back to Oriath: 24 zones, 18 bosses); **Shadow** (daggers, traps, stealth; Assassin, Infiltrator) and **Duelist**
  (swords, stances; Knight, Master-at-Arms); the passive tree rebuilt with jewel sockets inside the skill groups; 40 new uniques; the
  support gems rebalanced; a death screen that says what killed you.
- **League "Conquerors of Wraeclast"**: with Varek's mercenaries against the Blood Lord; recruit troops, take forts, build a Stronghold;
  23 army battles in the campaign, three factions and over a hundred castles in the endgame; troops are set in formation before a fight.
- **Accessibility: nothing announced.** No article or summary names a screen reader, audio options or controller changes; only players'
  forum threads ask for them.
- Unknown: whether early-access characters carry over, and which of the twelve classes are in at launch (ten are reported).

**For the mod at 1.0:** patch day ([patch day](../patch-day.md)) with a rebuilt passive tree (the build guide and the tree reader), two
classes' mechanics, two acts of quests, bosses and map hints, and a league whose formation screen is spatial.
