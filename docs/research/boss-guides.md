## CAMPAIGN ACT 1 (S1 unless noted, 0.2 data)

**The Bloated Miller** — The Riverbank
- Two-handed slam and charge are the threats. After dodging the slam you must also dodge the shockwave. The slam can one-shot a player with low max life. [S22: primagames/gamesradar snippets, plus Maxroll's general note that Act 1 boss slams can one-shot]

**The Brambleghast** — The Grelwood
- Volatile Slam can one-shot low max-life characters. [S1]
- Vine Screech spawns vines that must be killed. [S1]
- Volatile Vines are slow explosive balls to avoid. [S1]

**Beira of the Rotten Pack** — Clearfell
- Around 80% HP she adds Feeding Frenzy (revives undead wolves), Rotting Chill (frost runes that later become spikes) and Frost Swell (an ice ring). Shards forming on the ring show where the spikes expand. [S1, S4 for Isolde, whose Feeding Frenzy, Rotting Chill and Frost Swell have the same names]

**The Rotten Druid** — The Grim Tangle
- Rock Smash: dodge when the arm starts to throw down. [S1]
- Summon Vines: damage the boss to interrupt the channel. [S1]

**Asinia, The Praetor's Consort** — Tomb of the Consort
- Bone Storm: burst the bone wall quickly to escape. [S1]
- Death Runes: four runes under the player, the last larger and delayed. [S1]
- At about 75%, spirits appear. A spirit on a crumbling grave spawns an Eternal Knight, which reflects block damage. [S1]

**Draven, the Eternal Praetor** — Mausoleum of the Praetor
- Perishing Sunder: avoid cue, then a line stomp that widens sideways. [S1]
- At about 75%, spirits are dragged to him, with the same Eternal Knight rule as Asinia. [S1]

**Lachlann of Endless Lament** — Cemetery of the Eternals
- Earthquake is a cone slam that can one-shot low-life players. [S1]
- Spirit Burrow: a ghost chases, then spirals after you. [S1]
- Barrage and Spirit Eruption hinder for 2s and leave chilled ground. [S1]

**The King in the Mists** — Freythorn (also a Ritual pinnacle, see endgame). Data below is from the 0.2 guide. [S8, S1]
- Affliction Totem has low HP and its chaos damage over time grows with its lifetime. Kill it fast, before several stack.
- Ritual of Dance (purple): keep moving or you are rooted.
- Ritual of Meditation (red): stand still or you are rooted. You may roll once or twice, but too much movement roots you for several seconds.
- Thorngrip: purple vine cone, with a "Thorngrip!" voice line. Dodge past his side in melee, or roll away at range.
- Vine Slam: staff raised, red indicator over his head. Leave melee range.
- Mystic Fetish: he teleports to one of 3 fetishes and activates it, spawning Afflicted Swarms and Volatile Toads. Standing on fetish platforms stacks extra damage taken from Portent Vapors. Minions can destroy fetishes up the ramp.
- At 25% he becomes immune and sends you into the Wildwood Maze. Follow the glowing wisps to the exit (they dissipate after you move). Phase 2 then starts.
- Phase 2: a Blood Tornado spawns at phase start and chases you (armour break; a second one on +1). Blood Waves come from the arena edges with knockback. Explosive Spore is a large fast orb that maims, and pustules left behind detonate when walked over, so bait spores to the edges. Volatile Toads hop until they reach a player or a wall, then detonate.
- Phase 2's default attack fires 3 projectiles instead of 1.

**The Executioner** — Ogham Village
- Execution Sentence: glowing axe, then a large explosion. [S1]
- Guillotine: a falling blade after the lever pull. [S1]
- Leap Slam: jumps, then slams twice. [S1]
- Kill the soldier reinforcements to avoid being swarmed. [S1]

**Candlemass** — Ogham Manor
- Flame Breathe tracks you, Candle Mortar fires fireball volleys, and Leap Slam lands with a fiery AoE. [S1]

**Count Geonor** — Iron Citadel / Ogham Manor [S5 unless noted]
- Phase 1 moves:
  - Avalanche: frost walls appear, then an overhead slam shatters them. Dodge roll through.
  - Blood Burst: three blood spheres rupture in a large square ahead of him. Run past him (melee) or keep moving away (ranged).
  - Impale: sword pointed at you, then a charge that pins you to the wall with constant damage. Dodge it, then wait for his frost sweep before closing in.
  - Bloody Leap: he starts to dissipate, then slams on your position. Leave the circle early.
  - Frost Strike: sword pulled back, then three ice projectiles.
  - Wolf Form leaves a frost rune under the sword that freezes anyone who steps in.
  - Summon Pack: some werewolves explode on death.
- Around 25% he becomes Geonor, the Putrid Wolf.
- Phase 2:
  - Counter Attack: he starts spinning, then a large frost and physical arc if you are behind him. Dodge roll past him (melee) or away (ranged).
  - Leap Slam: leaps at you, then slams; leave the landing area.
  - Frozen Upheaval: ice patches spawn, then erupt. Run out of the radius.
  - Freezing Gale intermission: chilling mist fills the arena and werewolves keep spawning. He leaps roughly every 4 seconds, and a leap follows the end of each line of his dialogue. Dodge roll in cadence with the end of each line. The guide mentions a clearing in the centre of the mist but does not call it a safe spot.
- Pale Moon Requiem calls down pillars of chilling light. Blood Moon Requiem adds corrupting blood stacks and overlaps other attacks. The source gives the Blood Moon threshold as both about 30% and 50%, so verify in-game.
- [S22 sources disagree: one says about 40% with a full heal; Maxroll says about 25%. Prefer Maxroll.]

## CAMPAIGN ACT 3 (S2, 0.2 data)

**Mighty Silverfist** — Jungle Ruins
- Precise dodge rolls needed for his swing and slam, and the roll is lethal with low armour. Wait for openings.

**Ignagduk, the Bog Witch** — The Azak Bog
- Phase 1: bone spikes erupt from red runes, plus bone spears and bone walls.
- Below 75%: she ignites effigies and drops the broom.
- Infernal Corridor: the V-shaped fire exit blast is the key threat. Stand behind her or far from the exit.
- Flame Wall: two effigies send walls from several sides.

**Xyclucian, The Chimera** — Chimeral Wetlands
- Every 25% HP he flies to a stone pillar and volleys fireballs or poison needles. Breaking the stone platform stuns him. Hide behind pillars to block most ranged attacks.
- Ground Slam and Permafrost (a chill zone) are the main ground threats.

**Blackjaw, The Remnant** — Jiquani's Machinarium
- Enraged Slam gains new combos as his health drops. Flame Imbue (axe swing), Flamethrower (arc), Molten Lacerate (flame wall, projectiles, heavy strike).

**Zicoatl, Warden of the Core** — Jiquani's Sanctum
- Pyramid Slam: stand outside any corner of the triangle.
- Shocking Blast: roll through a laser gap, or run past the boss.
- Lightning Conduit: watch which wall fonts light up before the beams connect. Arcing Surge: avoid standing between connecting fonts. Lightning Wall and Laser Cascade: dodge the beams.

**Mektul, the Forgemaster** — The Molten Vault
- Timer: under 4 minutes before molten gold covers the aqueduct.
- Reheat: he runs back into the gold and fires waves. Pulling him too far from the gold makes him reset the wave phase early.
- Leap Slam triggers when you stand too far away. Volcanic Fissure: stalagmites explode as gold nears them or on his warcry. Explosive Rune: melee can move out before it explodes. Sunder: roll to the side when he starts stomping.

**Ketzuli, High Priest of the Sun** — Temple of Kopec
- Black Hole Sun: slow orbs, slowing you more the closer you are.
- Intermission: he goes invulnerable and flies up, firing fireballs and fast-spawning black holes.
- Enraged Dash: after landing he dashes and explodes. Dodge back in the direction he charged from. Dash count rises as HP falls.
- Gamma Ray: damage grows the longer you stay in the beam. Run toward him and roll past.

**Viper Napuatzi** — Utzaal (past)
- Arakaali's Sting: shield guards attack with spears. Stand opposite the side they can reach. After two spear phases the arena shrinks and both sides can hit you. When guards are poisoned, run into the wave and roll through it.
- Meteor Shower (around 75%): firestorm runes, then chaos meteors. Repeats as the arena shrinks.
- Serpent's Bite is unblockable; dodge to the side on her cue.
- Xopec's Spear: dodge the throw and the returning projectiles. Arakaali's Fury and Viper's Den: slam plus bouncing snakes.
- After her death, shield-wall stragglers must be cleared.

**Doryani, Royal Thaumaturge** — The Black Chambers (past)
- Mech phase (above 50%): ground slams, a frost laser arc and a flamethrower arc.
- Doryani's Fist: the mech becomes a giant fist that chases and slows you. Keep moving, then roll at the slam edge once it slows.
- Orbital Strike: small charge-up, dodge the beam. Static Blast: stand inside the static circle to avoid forked lightning. Burning Orbs: stay away; Flameblast detonates them.
- Vaal Frost Laser: roll past when the beam turns from blue to red. Soul Trap: avoid spike traps, roll if triggered.
- Ascension of the Vaal: lasers rotate clockwise. Stand on the right side before the turn.
- Self-destruct and the legs' laser: see boss-mechanics.md#doryani

Skip: Rootdredge, Queen of Filth ("isn't too difficult", but fungal chain explosions and adds; Maxroll says if you are caught in the Red when she attacks it is a potential one-shot per search summary [S22, unverified]).

## ACT 2 BOSSES (Maxroll interlude/Act-2-style page, S4's neighbour; source page title is "Act 2" content in S-series 0.2 data)

Bosses and what a player must answer. All from the one page I fetched under the 0.2 "Act 2" content list (the page that lists Rathbreaker through Jamanra). URL for this block: https://maxroll.gg/poe2/getting-started/act-2-boss-guide returned a result, but the fetch tool attributed it ambiguously; treat the specifics as 0.2 data.

**Rathbreaker** — Vastiri Outskirts
- Below about 60% he summons hyena packs; kill them fast. Drive By: a charge with falling spears beside him. After death, spearmen jump down from the cliffs.

**Rudja, the Dread Engineer** — Mawdun Mine
- Oil grenades slow you and add fire exposure. Ignition by flamethrower or explosive grenade makes burning ground.
- Scorched Earth: throws oil, then spins twice with the flamethrower, igniting all oiled ground. Stay off oil when she flames.
- Explosive Engineering: she chases you with a timed grenade. Drag her through an undetonated grenade to stagger her.

**Balbala, the Traitor** — Traitor's Passage
- Golden Barya: if the coin rune is left alone, a Shadow spawns. Stepping in triggers a slam with burning ground, so roll out immediately.
- Hide and Seek: poison gas spreads from the centre; find her to stop it, then roll away because she slams on discovery.
- Flicker Strike teleports behind you. Heavy chaos damage; overlevelling is recommended.

**Jamanra, the Risen King** — Halani Gates
- Flameblast has an audio cue. Run to the thrown sword and stand in the small circle.
- Obsidian Storm: leave the tornado radius before shards burst. Lightning Beam: left arm pulls back, then dodge.
- At 25%: sandstorm and Volatile Scarabs; survive the swarm to finish.

**Iktab & Ekbab** — Bone Pits
- Kill Iktab first; killing either empowers the other.
- Iktab: lightning runes strike shortly after appearing, three meteor runes, a central sphere that fires a projectile nova.
- After Iktab dies: a tracking sphere (explodes on contact) and a roaming sphere. After Ekbab dies: Iktab adds bone spikes, a larger meteor field and snaking lightning runes.

**Kabala, Constrictor Queen** — Keth
- Bone Cage: she walls off all but one entrance, then detonates the arena and applies a 20s Vulnerability. Find the open exit or burst through a wall.
- Snake Swarm from canopic jars. Death Nova fires explosive projectiles that detonate after a delay. Tail Whip reach grows as HP falls.

**Zalmarath, the Colossus** — Titan Grotto
- Roof Shatter drops rocks from the ceiling. Targeting is unreliable; aim at the hand if the body is hard to hit.
- Phase 2 (50%): Decimation Beam covers the arena, so stand behind the dagger. Enrage is repeated stabbing.
- At about 35%: flying constructs enter from the edges. Destroy them before they detonate on you.

**Azarian, the Forsaken Son** — Buried Shrines
- Arena shrinks quickly. Low fire resistance makes this very hard.
- Infernal Cyclone: a flaming spin toward you; time the dodge when he begins channelling. Burning Pitch: fire bombs create spreading burning ground in the corners.

**Tor Gul, the Defiler** — Spires of Deshar
- Flame Breath covers a third of the arena; direction (clockwise or counterclockwise) depends on your position. Flame Runes erupt around you.
- Below 50%: rune eruptions spawn flaming tornadoes, and Ribcage Barrage summons chasing skulls. Toxic Spew summons skeletons.

**Jamanra, the Abomination** — Dreadnaught Vanguard (Copper Citadel) [S6, plus the page above]
- Phase 1 (100% to 60%): beacon attacks. Beacon Toss throws two beacons that detonate in front of him, so run past the boss. Beacon Waltz forms a lightning wall that chases you; watch its speed early.
- Sandstorm (occasional): hide behind Sekhema Asala's shield. Zombies then rush you.
- Phase 2 (below 60%): tornadoes spawn and are re-summoned throughout. Obsidian Beacons replace Beacon Toss and slow you.
- Below 50%: Obsidian Shards call down lightning strikes.
- Below 25%: Obsidian Turbine, a ring storm firing a constant barrage of shards across the arena. This is the most punishing phase.
- Empowered Obsidian Storm: four tornadoes form shards that travel along a path. New air-whip attack knocks you back.
- High DPS stacks these mechanics and shrinks safe zones.
- [S22: one snippet says phase 2 starts around 50% and the boss stops taking damage at the transition; Maxroll says 60%. Prefer Maxroll.]

## ACT 4 (S3, 0.3 data)

**The Prisoner** — Abandoned Prison
- At 50% he is staggered: fire a ballista at him (three around the arena).
- At 25% an extra bolt applies corrupted blood. Blood Explosion is a large overhead AoE; Blood Tendrils track you. Frozen Upheaval and Glacial Cascade create ice spikes and shards to dodge.

**Scourge of the Skies** — Shrike Island
- Visceral Slam: avoid sound cue, leaves physical degeneration ground. Regurgitate: overlapping blood mortars. Rain of Viscera: tracking falling blood and bodies. Plagueling adds chase and bite until killed.

**Great White One** — Whakapanu Island
- Piranha Pools deal damage over time. Below 50% Sharknado: two tornadoes throw piranhas in a spiral.

**Diamora** — Singing Caverns
- Submerge double leap leaves waterspouts; stand near the arena entrance to avoid the leap. Tidal Crash and Drowning Orb have avoid cues.
- Below 60% Siren Song: a petrifying beam plus adds that rush you.

**Captain Hartlin** — Journey's End
- Anchor Slam: avoid cue, then a spectral anchor appears above you; dodge out. Cannon Barrage fires half-circle cannons in sequence. All Hands on Deck summons undead.

**Blind Beast** — Isle of Kin
- Fulminating Fissure: avoid cue, fissures detonate. Below 50% Sulphite haste makes his attacks faster.

**Krutog** — Volcanic Warrens
- Corpse Toss: overlapping bloody explosions. Regurgitate leaves a Sulphite puddle. Storm Walk: lightning stomps while he chases.

**Yama the White** — Halls of the Dead
- Monkey Business: dispel six clones at pillars, then he slams; roll away.
- Guessing Game: time stops, four clones appear, and you must find the safe zone. Boot movement speed is strongly recommended.
- Disengage: multiple rolls; pillars block projectiles. Eye Blast: caustic chaos ground. Chaos Flamethrower: roll past or away.

**Torvian** — Arastas
- Rock Toss: aim boulders at the three platforms to interrupt the priests buffing him.
- At about 25%: Beam of Order (rotating fire beam) and Radiating Order (avoid cue, axe explosions radiate out). Holy Barrier walls slow you.

**Benedictus** — The Excavation
- Earthen Maze: he teleports to the centre for a large blast. Stand in the safe zone, then leave the rock rings before the second explosion.
- Lightning Rods: boulders detonate if you get too close. Meteor Strike: avoid sound cue.
- At 50% the arena shrinks by about half. Earthen Maze returns with four walls to run through. Plasma Whip beams follow an avoid cue; roll or stand behind him.

**Tavakai, the Chieftain** — Heart of the Tribe
- Jade Slam: avoid cue plus Tukohama apparition; move away immediately. Jade Leap: the statue detonates after his landing slam. Jade Bomb: delayed explosion.
- Grappling Hook: avoid cue; if hit you are pulled in for multiple attacks. Summon Warrior Spirit: jade statues chase you.
- Invoke Ancestors (varies): Kitava marks you so his damage heals him; Tawhoa's plant growth reduces your damage outside the centre clearing; Valako creates a lightning-struck zone.
- At 50% Tavakai the Fallen leaves the arena and returns. Phase 2 adds Boiling Blood Volley (shock, ignite, fire resistance curse), blood puddles, a Jade Earthquake cone, and 3-5 statues active immediately.
- At 25% Tavakai the Consumed: Corrupted Sunder (avoid cue; crystals explode after a roar, find a safe spot), Seething Rage (move away from the leap slams), Corrupted Spikes (crystals detonate shortly after you walk over them).

## INTERLUDES (S4, 0.3 data)

**Isolde and Heldra** — Scorched Farmlands
- Isolde: Feeding Frenzy, Rotting Chill and Frost Swell as for Beira. Heldra's Infernal Corridor: stand behind her or far from the exit (V-shaped fire). Greater Bone Spike and Flame Wall punish standing still in marked areas.

**Siora, Blade of the Mists** — Stones of Serle
- Galvanic Reap: five lightning lines on a fixed pattern; use the gaps.
- Dark Mist: the arena darkens and lightning damage over time begins. Sprint to the glowing runed megalith.
- Raise Shade: invulnerable shades fire bolts from the edges. Lightning Storm: bolts strike the arena for heavy damage.

**Sigbert and Godwin** — Holten
- Ice Crash after the avoid cue; Frozen Upheaval runes detonate after a slam. Godwin's Lava Wave: three advancing waves, roll past him or stand between waves. Blast Furnace: leave the large circle quickly, or head to the church exit if space is tight.

**Oswin, the Dread Warden** — Wolvenhold
- Profane Flame stacks raise crit chance. Spike traps at the edges deal heavy physical and cold damage with high freeze chance. Ice Crash expands across the arena. Tempest Bell makes a cold zone that can freeze.

**Lady Elswyth and Thane Wulfric** — Holten Estate
- Engage Elswyth first; entering the arena can avoid activating Wulfric. Break the bone barrier (Bone Furnace) before her chaos-flame pillar lands. Wulfric's Bursting Sunder has an avoid cue and heavy damage. Wulfric enrages throughout.

**Vornas, the Fell Flame** — The Galai Gates
- Magma Fissure (avoid cue): multiple magma waves, then a fist explosion. At about 60% he is hasted.

**Akthi and Anundr** — The Khari Crossing
- Toxic Slam: when the boss turns green, move away from the head. Poison Barrage: after a red flash, roll through using invulnerability frames. At about 50% the tail joins; stay out of its sweep.

**Elzarah, the Cobra Lord** — Sel Khari Sanctuary
- Sand Mortar tracks your movement; Eruption is a line of ground eruptions; Tornado and Obsidian Storm fill the arena.

**Azmadi, the Faridun Prince** — Qimah Reservoir
- Sunder has little telegraph. Around 70% Zarokh stops time and grants him temporal powers.
- Mirage Charge can stun and kill: run far from the spawned swords or stand in the centre and be ready to dodge the charge.
- Wind Slash Combo: three empowered waves with little warning; dodge to the side. Temporal Slash: run out as the ring forms. Clockwork Rend: stand behind him.

**Lythara, the Wayward Spear** — Kriar Village
- Wildwood Wisps appear as her health drops, each granting a buff; fight far from them. Serpent's Bite is unblockable, so dodge to the side. Thunder Spear makes lightning storms.

**The Abominable Yeti** — Howling Caves: Tumble stuns repeatedly; Sunder is a two-swing combo into a heavy cold slam.

**Rakkar, the Frozen Talon** — Heart of the Tribe
- Frozen Slam and Wind Burst have avoid cues. Snowball: aim it at a wall to break it early. Falling Icicles and Ice Block walls throughout.

**Stormgore, the Guardian** — Etched Ravine
- Thunderous Rend: static wall, lightning projectiles, heavy strike. Charge Automaton: stone statues awaken and attack throughout.

**Zelina and Zolin** — The Cuachic Vault
- Zelina's Bloody Flicker Strike: a blood rune teleports her onto you while you stand in it, so leave the rune. Flurry becomes a blood bear slam.
- Zolin's Vaal Lightning: after he cuts the walls, beams fire from the arena's far end. His Blood Pool stacks corrupted blood.

## ENDGAME / PINNACLE (patch 0.5.x unless noted)

**Xesht, We That Are One** — Breach pinnacle [S7; 0.5 roster in S21]
- Breach timer: killing monsters adds time; at zero you lose the fight. Loot dropping as the timer vanishes means you are near the arena.
- Hand Smash is the hardest hit. Watch the purple/pink hand telegraph and dodge to the side of the hand (wrist/finger side). Track its shadow while it hovers.
- Tul icicles (cold, can freeze; thawing charm recommended). Esh lightning ring expands to a shockwave; dodge toward the hand or stand outside. Combo Summon does both at once.
- Burrow: he goes invulnerable and chases you, then claps and slices the ground both ways. Circle the inner arena edge and stay out until the slices end.
- Finger Gun: slow wind-up with a sound cue. A wall blocks it, a Frost Wall does not. Triple Blast: shoulders glow purple, then 3 sets of double shots after a sound cue.
- Xoph Sun Orb: walk away from the direction he faces.
- About 50%: he stops, a voice line plays, a purple portal appears above him (once). This is the safest damage window.
- Phase 2: Arm Smashes from floor portals slam in a line after a delay. Outrange or dodge once the elbow starts bending. Portal count rises to 4 at difficulty 4 and they get faster. Arm Smashes overlap every other mechanic.
- Source date not stated on the page; written when Maxroll listed difficulty tiers (difficulty 4). Check against 0.5.

**Olroth, Origin of the Fall** — Expedition pinnacle (Logbook, area level 79+) [S10, S21]
- Starfire Beam: "Starfire" voice line, a massive beam crosses the arena centre.
- Extermination Beam: tracking beam that follows you. Starfall Carousel: chilling beams fall and contract/expand in a circle.
- Starfall Boomerang: avoid cue. He throws the sword across the arena and it returns, then a large cold AoE explodes around him. A hit in flight applies Hinder.
- Impale: point-first charge, then a wide arc slash. Roll mid-charge to avoid the follow-up.
- Summon Reinforcements: portals spawn skeletons; armoured skeletons slam. On some difficulties the portals close at revival.
- After the first kill he revives at about 70% HP.

**King in the Mists / The Bodach** — Ritual pinnacle [S21, S8]
- Same fight as the Freythorn boss above at lower tiers. At tier 14+ it escalates into The Bodach; no Bodach mechanics found in any source. Sources disagree on whether it is Ritual or Delirium (S22). The 0.5.4 notes said the King was nerfed (details not found).

**The Simulacrum: Omniphobia and Kosis** — Delirium [S21, S22]
- Wave count conflicts: most sources say 15 waves; one boosting blog says 7 in 0.5. Unresolved.
- Omniphobia: heavy axe swings, earth slam and leap slam (the main burst threat), plus an Axe Slash Projectile. Spawn: from wave 10, guaranteed at wave 15 per one database entry (S22). Can also appear on maps with active Delirium.
- Kosis: charges energy and detonates it as large AoE explosions. His Ice Beam or teleport leaves Ice Orbs that chill and deal damage over time when you stand near; strip his energy shield quickly to stop the orbs. Spawns from wave 15 per the same entry.
- Fighting both with simulacrum wave modifiers and stacked delirium debuffs is rated very dangerous (S22). No phase detail found.

**Zarohk, the Temporal** — Trial of the Sekhemas (spelled Zarokh elsewhere) [S9]
- About 75% HP: Temporal Tendrils. He teleports to the centre, spawns time traps, rotates counterclockwise firing tendrils. Repeats for the rest of the fight. Safest spot is the top corner (1-2 hits, no mines to dodge); alternatively stand near the volley's centre and roll through. Too close risks a shotgun hit.
- About 40%: Temporal Mines spawn periodically and overlap other attacks.
- Temporal Orb: "avoid cue" sound. It detonates on reaching you; body-block with minions, totems or a frost wall.
- Temporal Rift: a backwards-winding clock teleports you to the oldest mirage where a rift explodes. Avoid the explosion.
- Sands of Time (highest lethality): he vanishes and the room centre glows. Entering starts a timer and hourglasses spawn around the arena. You must reach every hourglass in time, and movement slows the longer you stay. Missing one: he returns, freezes you, then kills you instantly. 30-35% movement speed boots recommended; Blink helps.
- Does not scale with the Atlas tree; difficulty comes only from Trial relics. Last Flame Incense sets Honour to 1 (no hits allowed) for the Temporalis challenge [S21].

**The Trialmaster** — Trial of Chaos [S11, 0.2 data; S22]
- With spear: Branching Sunder (overhead slam); Blood Eruption (plants spear, large explosion after an avoid sound cue); Chaos Spear (thrown, expands a physical damage-over-time zone around where it lands); Blood Wave Whirlwind (spins and fires crescent waves; a shield blocks the spin, not the crescents).
- Without spear: three lightning, cold and fire orbs that explode on impact; Blood Orb is fast, bounces off walls, duplicates and breaks armour.
- Tether (either state): spawns a heart at a random spot. Entering its circle slows and tethers you. Leaving the circle breaks the chain, stuns you and detonates the heart.
- Ultimatum (either state): he says "Ultimatum" or "Entropy" before time freezes. With spear he slams several times, creating branching sunders that burst when time resumes; roll to a safe spot after unfreeze. Without spear he teleports around you three times and spawns a Blood Orb (the guide also says three orbs, so it contradicts itself).
- Does not scale with the Atlas tree. No one-shot values, phase thresholds or adds given. Hugging the arena walls helps (Mobalytics snippet, S22).

**Trial of Chaos bosses** (reached before the Trialmaster)
- Chetza, the Feathered Plague [S18]: falling bird corpses explode into blood and "can easily one-shot you"; spiral of bone spikes applies Maim; lasers and pulses force constant movement. A Plagueling add spawns at 50% and near death, and it must be killed to finish the fight.
- Uxmal, the Beastlord [S19]: landing stomp (may repeat), fire breath from the air, fireball, poison volleys. Breath patches (lightning, ice, fire by level) persist until the fight ends, so it is a race against time. He flies off to other parts of the map.
- Bahlak, the Sky Seer [S22, Game8/Deltia snippets]: turns into a large tornado and spawns smaller ones; later patches added a small push away from tornadoes. Dark-sky phase sends long lines of bats. Flies off-screen and slams on your position. Rated the hardest of the three; forum posts claim 25-30% movement speed is required (anecdotal). No adds.

**The Arbiter of Ash** — Burning Monolith, inside the Precursor Fortress in 0.5 [S12, S17, S21, S22]
- Access: three Crisis Fragments (Ancient, Faded, Weathered). About 5M HP, over 22M in the Uber version.
- Phase 1 (100% to 55%). Red flash plus sound cue marks attacks that must be dodged.
  - Fire projectiles are slow bolts. Fire Beam: a hovering claw-scepter, a sustained beam; move perpendicular.
  - Bullet Hell Orbs: two fire orbs travel in a line.
  - Flame Seed: a seed lands in a marked circle; stand inside the circle, everything else explodes.
  - Cascading Flame Seeds: he dashes and drops seeds that blast in sequence; hop between safe circles after each blast.
  - Hallways: he teleports and does arena-wide flame blasts with one gap, "slightly bluish"; find the gap and move there fast.
  - Do not reach 55% while a seed or cascade is mid-animation.
- Phase 2 (below 55%): he picks up his weapon from the arena centre.
  - Combo Attack: two- or three-hit sword combo with a red flash.
  - Fire and Ice: black and white orbs spawn; black leaves burning ground, white leaves chilling ground; step on the opposite orb to cancel. He may stab an orb to empower his weapon.
  - Sword Beam: sword spin, then a fire line. Pulsing Orb: expanding fire rings that chain-explode on contact.
  - Diving Slam: flies up, fire line across the arena, then slams on the landing zone.
  - Baiting Flame Seeds (hardest): two seeds in front of him, then Sword Beam and Ground Sunder. Wait near the circle until he thrusts forward, then step in; stand beside the circle for the second seed so the narrow Sunder line misses.
  - Phase 2 combos and Diving Slam can take over 50% of life.
- One-shots: a player forum post says the one-shots were in phase 1 (circle blasts with tight telegraph timing, especially when the next circle spawns far from the last) and that phase 2 was easy. One reply suggests the one-shots happen only at set HP checkpoints. Opinion, not official. The fire-alley (Hallways) phase has poor visibility.
- Fire is the main lethal type; 75% fire resistance cap advised. A change gave up to five respawn attempts and longer invulnerability while he emerges (per the wiki, as quoted in S22).

**The Arbiter of Divinity** — Origin Tower, 0.5.4 [S13]
- Phase 1 (above about 40%): fast spear duelist. Dashing Spear Thrust (side-roll through the dash), Upper Spear Slash (roll out during wind-up), Golden Charged Lunge (dodge perpendicular), AoE Divine Slam (leave the marked zone), Heavy Combo (roll the slam, then the spin), Ranged Combo (two slashes, then a beam), Divine Electrical Orbs (slow lightning orbs), Aerial Assault.
- Phase 2 (about 40%): faster, adds Super Dash Thrust (roll as soon as it telegraphs), Triple Spinning Slash, Rain of Divine Lightning, and spawns Aspects of Divinity (mirror images).
- Divine Power orbs: collecting one grants temporary immunity and destroys the Aspects. Save one for when the Aspects appear.
- No timings or one-shot thresholds in the source.

**Vessel of Kulemak** — Abyss pinnacle, Well of Souls / Black Cathedral (sources conflict on the location) [S15, S16]
- Structure: you kill the Vessel, pick one of three petrified Liches (Amanamu, Ulaman, Kurgal) and revive it stronger. Revival buffs include faster attacks, an extra wave of minions, or an added damage type; the cycle they apply in is not given. Ending choice: take the finger (3-modifier ring) or return it and fight the Vessel once more at Full Strength.
- The Invitation is consumed on entry and is not refunded on death.
- Skill data (S15, base values, level 79, about 64.6k life): Teleport Slam bypasses dodge roll and block, +80 attack distance, 25s cooldown, with up to 33% extra damage at the slam centre; Cold Cleave and Green Cleave can be dodge-rolled; Totem Laser and Mega Beam apply shock; Proximity Mine triggers on proximity; Volatile Ground does 735 physical per second with infinite duration once created. Asset names hint at a shrink ring and degeneration effect (inferred, not confirmed).
- No guide-level phase detail found.

**Atziri, the Red Queen** — Atziri's Vault, Vaal Temple [S14, S20, S22]
- Two forms: the Red Queen, then the Fell Serpent, with a scripted story transition between them (S22).
- Headdress horns ignite to telegraph her next attack school (fire or lightning); lightning horns signal projectile bursts (S14, S22).
- Blood Spear: thrown spear lands and explodes into about 50 projectiles with Corrupted Blood and Maim; do not stand on the marker, roll out as it lands.
- Spinning Flame Wall: full swing leaves a ring of fire; step out of range during wind-up and do not path back through.
- Flameblast: a channel on a marked area, then it detonates; leave the zone. Empowered Flameblast: two overlapping red runes explode after a short delay; stand outside both. Greedy Flameblast hits are what most often kill players.
- Burning Circle: a flame circle around her; crossing it deals heavy damage. Fight from outside at range or stay tight inside; do not straddle the edge.
- Phase 2: faster overlapping zones; Atziri lights columns with red blood that bleeds (S22); around 20% in phase 2 an attack covers the whole arena in red AoE (S22, one snippet, uncorroborated).
- Highest base hits: Blood Cannon (up to 2755 physical) and empowered Flameblast (up to 2583 fire); real hits are higher (S20). Cap fire resistance at 75%.
- Access: the arena door stays locked until you interact with the Royal Access Chamber (S22). Atziri's Medallion drops on defeat.
